Hi, I’m the founder of Frida.

First of all, thank you so much for being here. This is my first app launch, so I’m currently experiencing the very special combination of excitement and nervousness.

I’m a huge fan of minimal interfaces and personal agents like Poke & Instinct.

Frida definitely takes inspiration from that direction, but we’re trying to carve out our own path rather than just making “Poke, but with a different font.”

There are also areas where, frankly, we’re not quite there yet, especially browser capabilities and agentic payments. We know. We see it. The comparison is not exactly flattering.

But here’s what I can promise.

Frida will get dramatically better, week by week. Think x5, not 5%. There will be rough edges, weird moments, and probably a few things that make you wonder what I was thinking.

So if you don’t love it at first, please give us a few chances before you delete it.

We’ll keep building until you fall in love.

And if you still delete it after that… ouch.

Genuinely, thank you for being one of my first users.

Alex Yang

Founder, MonyCompany Inc
