About

We build interfaces with minimalistic complexity.

Computer-use agents are making software increasingly capable, but most interfaces still force people to operate through rigid workflows, buttons, menus, and predefined interactions.

We believe the interface should become simpler as the underlying system becomes more powerful.

Instead of exposing the complexity of a computer-use agent, we give it a minimal environment where it can understand context, navigate software, execute tasks, and work alongside people. The complexity lives underneath the interface, not inside it.
